# 正文（Description 字段，以下整段复制）

**事物的语义开始漂移，形态随之改变。**
**When the name of a thing changes, the thing changes with it.**

## English

The observer array went offline on 10/02/2021. Since then the semantics of things drift: a stone block becomes something else, a cow becomes another creature, and the weather stops being reasonable. It only gets faster.

**Focal Decay** is a Minecraft mod built on *SCP-CN-2999 "Observator Ex Machina"*. The decay is not a visual trick — interact with what you see and it really does become that thing. Mine the diamond ore in front of you and diamonds come out, even if there was dirt there a moment ago. The collapse is tearing your base apart and handing you the best resource vein you will ever find.

### What you do

- **Stabilise a patch of the world** — Place an Observer Base, insert an OBSR model, and everything in range is locked in its current form. Inside the field nothing drifts. Outside it, everything does.
- **Train a classifier** — Put a blank OBSR prototype in a Training Terminal and pick a direction, carry it out to right-click blocks and record targets, then return to finish. The terminal resolves a *concept* and its completeness.
- **Pick a model** — Three of the five OBSR specifications are trained: **Semantic Lock** protects recorded targets, **Guided Mutation** steers where the drift goes, and the **Candidate Observer** trains itself. **Bio Stabiliser** and **Total Stability** are built or earned instead.
- **Collect seven Semantic Fragments** — One per event from the previous iteration. Every one comes from a one-off milestone, not a random chest.
- **Reach the Throne** — A structure in the void belt of the End. The enthronement ritual activates an OBSR-EX; installing a completed Candidate Observer at the Observer Core ends the defocus for good.
- **Read the manual** — A field manual is issued on first join. With Patchouli installed it is a full in-fiction archive of the previous iteration.

In-game text is available in both English and Chinese.

## 中文

观测者阵列于 10/02/2021 下线。此后，事物的语义开始漂移：一块石头会变成别的东西，一只牛会变成别的生物，天气也开始不讲道理。而且它只会越来越快。

**《失焦》** 是一个基于 *SCP-CN-2999《Observator Ex Machina》* 的 Minecraft 模组。失焦不是视觉把戏——你看得见什么，碰上去就真的会变成什么。挖一块看起来是钻石矿的方块，掉出来的就是钻石，哪怕那里原本是泥土。这场崩坏一边拆你的房子，一边把最好的矿脉送到你面前。

### 你要做的事

- **稳住一片地方** —— 放下观测者基座，插进一枚 OBSR 模型，范围内的一切就被锁在当下的形态。范围里什么都不变，范围外什么都在变。
- **训练一个分类器** —— 空白原型放进训练终端指定方向，带出去右键方块记录目标，回到终端完成训练。终端会解析出一个*概念*和它的完备度。
- **挑一枚型号** —— 五种 OBSR 里有三种需要训练：**语义锁定**保护已记录的目标，**引导突变**决定漂移的去向，**候选观测者**则自己训练自己。**生物稳定**与**完全稳定**不走训练这条路。
- **收集七枚语义碎片** —— 一枚对应上一迭代的一件旧事。全部来自一次性里程碑，不做随机掉落。
- **走到王座** —— 末地虚空带里的结构。登座仪式激活 OBSR-EX；在观测者核心装上已完成的候选观测者，失焦终止。
- **翻那本手册** —— 首次进入世界自动发放。装上 Patchouli 后，它是上一迭代留档的完整基金会档案。

游戏内文本中英双语。
